### Changed
- **Audiobookshelf import stops re-asking Hardcover about books it already matched** (#2173). A repeat import now skips the Hardcover series match for items it linked before and for items it searched and found nothing for, so a big library no longer re-walks thousands of searches every run. A miss is retried after about a month, and an item whose title, author or series changed in Audiobookshelf is matched again straight away. Skipped items also skip the upstream metadata re-merge and linking of volumes Hardcover adds later, so send `"full": true` when you want those refreshed. `POST /api/v1/abs/import` also takes an optional `libraryIds` list to import just some of your configured libraries, and `"full": true` to force a complete re-match.
