### Fixed
- **Search no longer approves the next book in a series** (#3268): a release that names your book and then goes on to name another one, such as "King of Scars 02 - Rule of Wolves" for "King of Scars" or "[Rotten School 12] - Battle of the Dum Diddys" for "Rotten School", is now dropped from automatic and interactive search, with the reason shown in the search debug view. A later series number is caught when the book has a stored series position (including roman numerals such as "III"), and another of the author's books is caught when it is in your library. A release that names the book's own series is not mistaken for another book.
- **A main title no longer stands in for a book with a subtitle** (#3268): "Legion (2012)" or "The High Druid of Shannara 3 - Straken" is no longer approved for "Legion: Skin Deep" or "High Druid of Shannara: Jarka Ruus" when another result of the same format carries the full title, when the main title is another of the author's books, or when the release names a different series number. A summary or study guide never hides the real release, and the plain "Author - Title" release of a subtitled book is kept at any series position, unless that main title is itself another of the author's books in your library that is not the book's series.
- **Single chapters of an audiobook are filtered out of search** (#3268): a chapter marker ("Ch63", "Chapter 12") or an "NN of MM" counter with MM of 10 or more is dropped like other raw usenet postings unless the release names an ebook format or a series count word ("Book 3 of 12"). A whole book "Part 1 of 2" and any counter with MM under 10 are kept. Reported by Broomfundel and foobarbigtime.
