### Fixed
- **Search no longer approves the next book in a series** (#3268) - a release that names your book and then goes on to name another one, such as "King of Scars 02 - Rule of Wolves" for "King of Scars" or "[Rotten School 12] - Battle of the Dum Diddys" for "Rotten School", is now dropped from automatic and interactive search, with the reason shown in the search debug view. Bindery uses the book's series position and the author's other titles in your library when it has them.
- **A main title no longer stands in for a book with a subtitle** (#3268) - "Legion (2012)" or "The High Druid of Shannara 3 - Straken" is no longer approved for "Legion: Skin Deep" or "High Druid of Shannara: Jarka Ruus" when another result carries the full title, when the book is a later entry in its series, or when the release names a different series number.
- **Single chapters of an audiobook are filtered out of search** (#3268) - posts such as "Faith of the Fallen Ch63 of 71.mp3" are dropped like other raw usenet postings. "Part 1 of 2" style releases are kept. Reported by Broomfundel and foobarbigtime.
