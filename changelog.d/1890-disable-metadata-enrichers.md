### Added
- **Turn metadata sources off one by one** (#1890, #2015). Settings, Metadata now has a checkbox for each enricher (OpenLibrary, Hardcover, Google Books and DNB) and for the two audiobook sources, the Audible catalogue and audnex. Untick one and Bindery stops using it as an enricher (the two audiobook sources are not asked at all), so you can skip DNB if you read no German, or leave Amazon's unpublished Audible endpoint alone. It applies to the next lookup with no restart. Everything stays on by default, and the primary provider can never be turned off. Turning a source off means less fill in, for example DNB also supplies covers and Hardcover also supplies genres, series and edition language. Thanks to EricSilberberg for the ask.
