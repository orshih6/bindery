### Added
- **Progress for the catalogue fetch after a Readarr import** (#2136). Once the database import finished, Bindery went on fetching every new author's books in the background with nothing on screen to say so, which looked like a stuck import on a big library. The Import page now follows the import itself and then shows "Fetching catalogues: X of Y" until it is done, and picks the line back up if you leave and return. The pace is unchanged at about 3 seconds per author, so 500 authors take around 25 minutes. A restart clears the count. Thanks to gchahcg for the report.
