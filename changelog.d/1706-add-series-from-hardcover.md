### Added
- **Search Hardcover and add a series in one step** (#1706). With enhanced Hardcover data on, the Add Series box on the Series page now has a search. Type a title, press Enter, and every result shows its author, how many books it has and the first few titles, so near-identical series are easy to tell apart. Pick one and Bindery creates the series already linked to Hardcover and opens it with its list of missing books. Nothing is added to your library until you choose, one book at a time or with Fill gaps. If that Hardcover series is already in your library you land on the existing one instead of getting a second copy. Typing a plain name still works as before. Thanks to Aiz0 for asking for this in discussion #1682, and to Noxxiss for the idea of previewing a series before importing it.
